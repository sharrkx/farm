Mindrift

Mindrift is a popular-science short-video channel (YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok).

The "Mindrift Analytics" app is an internal tool used only by the channel owner. It reads the channel's own YouTube statistics (views, watch time, likes) in read-only mode to decide which topics to make next. It does not post, edit or delete anything and is not offered to other users.
